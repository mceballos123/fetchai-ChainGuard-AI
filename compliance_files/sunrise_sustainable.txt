Sunrise partners with Amazonian communities practicing agroforestry. Coffee grown alongside native fruit trees and hardwoods. Farmers paid 35% above market rates with long-term contracts providing financial stability. No intermediaries - direct relationships between roasters and farming families. Technical support for organic certification transition provided at no cost to farmers.

Women's cooperatives supported with microloans and business training. Revenue sharing model gives communities 10% equity stake. Anti-discrimination policies protect LGBTQ+ workers and farmers.
