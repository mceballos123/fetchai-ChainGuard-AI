Supplier Name: Green Valley Coffee Co.
Location: Costa Rica
Product: Arabica Coffee Beans

Farm Practices:
Green Valley Coffee Co. works directly with 45 small family-owned farms in the Tarrazu region. All partner farms are certified Fair Trade and Rainforest Alliance. Farmers receive prices 25% above Fair Trade minimum, ensuring sustainable livelihoods.

Employee Treatment:
Farm workers receive living wages that exceed regional standards by 40%. All employees have access to free healthcare, paid sick leave, and maternity/paternity benefits. Children of farm workers receive educational scholarships funded by the cooperative. Workers have union representation and participate in profit-sharing programs.

Sustainability Practices:
100% organic farming methods with zero synthetic pesticides. Water conservation through drip irrigation systems reduces water usage by 60%. Solar panels provide 80% of processing facility energy. Coffee pulp is composted and returned to soil. Shade-grown coffee protects biodiversity and bird habitats.

Ethical Values:
Transparent supply chain with full traceability from farm to export. Annual third-party audits verify working conditions and environmental practices. 5% of profits invested in local community projects including schools and clean water initiatives. Gender equality programs support women farmers with leadership training and access to land ownership.
