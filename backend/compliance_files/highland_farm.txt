
Supplier Name: Highland Heritage Farms
Location: Ethiopia
Product: Single-Origin Arabica Coffee

Farm Practices:
Highland Heritage works with indigenous farming communities in the Sidamo and Yirgacheffe regions. Direct trade relationships eliminate middlemen, ensuring farmers retain 70% of final sale price. Traditional heirloom varieties are preserved and cultivated using ancestral knowledge passed down through generations.

Employee Treatment:
Workers are primarily family members and community members with multi-generational ties to the land. Fair wages are supplemented with housing, meals during harvest, and year-round employment opportunities including processing and packaging. Seasonal workers receive 50% higher rates than industry standard. On-site childcare allows parents to work without family separation.

Sustainability Practices:
Natural forest coffee cultivation preserves native ecosystems. Zero deforestation policy with reforestation programs planting 10 trees for every 1 harvested. Natural spring water used in processing, returned clean to watersheds. Parchment and husks used as fuel for drying, creating closed-loop system. Wildlife corridors maintained throughout farms.

Ethical Values:
Preserving indigenous culture and traditional farming knowledge. Women comprise 60% of management positions in processing facilities. Transparent pricing shared openly with buyers. Revenue reinvested in community infrastructure including medical clinics and literacy programs. Support for local artisans and craftspeople through cultural preservation funds.

Certifications: Fair Trade, Organic, Direct Trade Verified, Smithsonian Bird Friendly
