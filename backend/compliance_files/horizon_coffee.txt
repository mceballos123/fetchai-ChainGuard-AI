Supplier Name: Horizon Coffee Collective
Location: Guatemala
Product: Shade-Grown Arabica

Farm Practices:
Horizon is a women-led cooperative of 80 small farms in the Antigua and Huehuetenango regions. Women farmers have full ownership and control of production decisions. Quality-based pricing rewards careful cultivation with premiums up to 60% over commodity rates. Crop diversification training reduces dependence on single commodity.

Employee Treatment:
Equal pay for equal work regardless of gender - rare in the industry where women typically earn 30% less. On-site nursery and preschool allows working parents to keep children nearby. Literacy programs for workers who missed formal education. Zero-tolerance sexual harassment policy with independent reporting mechanisms. Worker councils meet monthly to address concerns and make recommendations. Emergency fund provides interest-free loans for family emergencies.

Sustainability Practices:
Bird-friendly shade coffee preserves migratory bird habitats. Organic composting systems eliminate need for chemical fertilizers. Solar-powered processing facilities with rainwater harvesting. Coffee cherry cascara upcycled into tea products reducing waste. Bee corridors and native flowering plants support pollinators. Soil health monitoring and regenerative practices build topsoil.

Ethical Values:
Gender equity as core mission with leadership training for women. Domestic violence support services and safe housing for survivors. Educational scholarships specifically for daughters of farmers. Partnership with local NGOs fighting gender-based discrimination. Transparent pricing and profit sharing with detailed annual reports. Investment in girls' education through community schools.

Certifications: Fair Trade Women-Owned, Organic, Bird Friendly, Certified B Corp
