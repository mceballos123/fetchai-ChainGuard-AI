RISK MANAGEMENT PROFILE: Sunrise Sustainable

SUPPLIER OVERVIEW:
Sunrise Sustainable is a vertically integrated renewable energy equipment manufacturer based in Morocco, producing solar panels, wind turbine components, and energy storage systems.

CAPACITY CONSTRAINTS:
- Current Production Capacity: 150 MW equivalent components/month, operating at 70% utilization
- Seasonal Demand: Peak season (March-August) drives 60% of annual revenue
- Manufacturing Facility Constraints: Single automated facility at capacity; expansion would take 18-24 months
- Raw Material Processing: Chemical processing for panel materials creates bottleneck; 3-4 week lead times
- Inventory Management: Warehouse capacity 500 units; seasonal demand creates stockpile challenges

NATURAL DISASTER RISKS:
- Flooding Risk: Located in region with seasonal flooding
- Sand Storms: Saharan dust storms affect operations March-May
- Extreme Heat: Temperatures exceed 45°C regularly; cooling system failures possible
- Water Scarcity: Arid climate; water availability for manufacturing increasingly uncertain

LOGISTICS & ACCESSIBILITY:
- Port Access: 150km to Mediterranean ports; shipping to Europe 3-5 days, North America 10-14 days
- Road Infrastructure: Recently upgraded, but political issues could impact trade routes
- Transportation Costs: Moderate costs; fuel price volatility affects bottom line 5-8% quarterly

FINANCIAL RISKS:
- Commodity Price Volatility: Silicon prices fluctuate 20-25% annually
- Currency Risk: Export revenue in EUR and USD; Moroccan Dirham fluctuations 8-12% quarterly
- Working Capital: Seasonal revenue creates cash flow challenges September-February
- Debt Obligations: 40% debt-to-equity ratio

MARKET RISKS:
- Customer Concentration: Top 5 customers represent 55% of revenue; customer loss critical
- Chinese Competition: Aggressive pricing creates margin pressure

RECOMMENDATIONS:
1. Diversify customer base
2. Establish strategic partnerships with European manufacturers
3. Develop supply chain redundancy for critical raw materials
4. Build cash reserves to weather seasonal demand variations
